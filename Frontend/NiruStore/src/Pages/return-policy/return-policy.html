Return Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Return Eligibility

Products may be eligible for return if they are unused, undamaged and returned with the original packaging and accessories.

Return Period

Return requests should be submitted within the applicable return period shown on the product or order details.

Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene, safety or other product-specific restrictions.

Refunds

Once an eligible return has been received and inspected, the refund process will be initiated according to the original payment method.

Damaged Products

If your order arrives damaged or incorrect, please contact customer support as soon as possible with your order details and relevant photographs.

Contact Support

For return-related questions, please contact NiruStore customer support.
