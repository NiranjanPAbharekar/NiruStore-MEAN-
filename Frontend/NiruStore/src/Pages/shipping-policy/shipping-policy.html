Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shipping Information

At NiruStore, we work to ensure your order reaches you safely and on time. Orders are processed after successful payment confirmation.

Processing Time

Orders are generally processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays may be processed on the next business day.

Delivery Time

Standard delivery usually takes approximately 3–7 business days depending on your location.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed during checkout before you complete your order.

Delayed Orders

Delivery may occasionally be delayed due to weather, holidays, courier issues or circumstances outside our control.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding your shipment, please contact our customer support team.
